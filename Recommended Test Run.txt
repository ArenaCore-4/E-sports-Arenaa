# ArenaCore – Complete Sample Test Run

## 1. Player Registration
Register 14 players with unique IDs:

- P01 to P07
- P08 to P14

Enter their names and verify that all players are registered successfully.

## 2. Team Management
Create two teams:

- **T01 – Alpha:** P01, P02, P03, P04, P05, P06, P07
- **T02 – Bravo:** P08, P09, P10, P11, P12, P13, P14

Search for both teams and verify their player details.

Test updating a player's information and searching for a player using their ID.

## 3. Tournament Management
Create a tournament with the following details:

- Tournament ID: TR01
- Tournament Name: Arena Championship
- Game: Valorant
- Entry Fee: 1000
- Team Capacity: 8

Register Alpha and Bravo for the tournament.

Verify that both teams appear in the registered teams list.

## 4. Match Scheduling
Schedule a match between Alpha and Bravo.

Verify that the match is assigned a unique match ID and appears in the scheduled matches list.

## 5. Results and Leaderboard
Enter the match result:

- Alpha: 13
- Bravo: 8

Verify that Alpha is declared the winner and that the team statistics and leaderboard points are updated correctly.

## 6. Reports and Statistics
Open the Reports and Statistics module.

Check the available reports, including:

- Tournament standings
- Match history
- Team statistics
- Player statistics

Verify that the displayed information matches the registered records and match results.

## 7. Save and Load Data
Open the Save/Load Data module.

1. Select Save Data.
2. Verify that `ArenaCore.dat` is created.
3. Exit the program.
4. Run ArenaCore again.
5. Select Load Data.
6. Search for the previously registered players, teams, tournament, and match.

Verify that all saved information is restored successfully.

## 8. Error Handling
Test the following invalid operations:

- Registering a player with an existing ID.
- Creating a team with an existing team ID.
- Adding a player who is already assigned to another team.
- Entering an invalid player or team ID.
- Selecting an invalid tournament capacity.
- Scheduling a match with an invalid team.
- Entering an invalid match result.
- Selecting an invalid menu option.

Verify that the program displays appropriate error messages and does not accept invalid operations.

## 9. Exit
Return to the main menu and enter `0` to terminate the program.

**Expected Outcome:** ArenaCore should successfully manage players, teams, tournaments, match scheduling, results, reports, and data storage while handling invalid operations appropriately.
